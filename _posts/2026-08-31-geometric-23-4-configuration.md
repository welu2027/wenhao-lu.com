In 2000, Branko Grünbaum proposed the question of which $n$ admit a geometric $(n_4)$ configuration, a set of $n$ points and $n$ straight lines in the real projective plane where each point lies on exactly four of the lines and each line passes through exactly four of the points. Examples were known for each $n \ge 18$ except $n = 23$, and no proof of nonexistence existed either.

In my research, I found two $(23_4)$ configurations, thus closing the existence problem. Together with the known constructions, this thus proves that a geometric $(n_4)$ configuration exists <strong>if and only if $n \ge 18$ and $n \ne 19$</strong>. I also show why this evaded prior researchers, because any geometric $(23_4)$ configuration has at most four projective symmetries, which puts it out of reach of the symmetric constructions that settled every other value.
