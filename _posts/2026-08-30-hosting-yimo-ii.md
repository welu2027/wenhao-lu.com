What better way to end off the summer than hosting [YIMO](https://yimo-official.org/) II (August 29-30)?

YIMO (pronounced _yee-mo_), the Youth International Math Olympiad, is a free math olympiad built by students, for students. Our motivation was that outside the national olympiad pipeline, most countries have nothing like the circuit we are fortunate to have in the US, with contests like PUMaC and HMMT. I cofounded YIMO with [Ryan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ryan-ahn-81322736a/) and [Jun](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hyunjun-yi-3424573a0/) in March 2026, with just an idea and the belief that a competition like this could reach kids in every country and bring equitable access to competitive math. Now, less than six months later, across our two events we have had over **3,000 participants** from around the world. Thank you to HRT, PiMath, and AoPS for sponsoring, to all the students who participated, and to all the dedicated staff who made it possible.
