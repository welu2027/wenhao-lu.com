A geometric $(n_4)$ configuration is a set of $n$ points and $n$ straight lines in the real projective plane where every point lies on exactly four of the lines and every line passes through exactly four of the points. Which $n$ admit one has been open at a single value for years: examples were known for every $n \ge 18$ except $n = 23$, and no proof of nonexistence existed either.

<div class="img-duo">
  <p class="duo-text">I found two $(23_4)$ configurations, thus closing the existence problem. Together with the known constructions, a geometric $(n_4)$ configuration exists <strong>if and only if $n \ge 18$ and $n \ne 19$</strong>. I also bound the projective symmetry group of a geometric $(23_4)$ configuration by four and classify the Klein-symmetric case exactly. Read the preprint on <a href="https://zenodo.org/records/22683569" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Zenodo</a>.</p>
