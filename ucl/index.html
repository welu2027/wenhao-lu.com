    How it works

    1. 411 motion-captured pitches → physics → elbow torque (matches the lab, r = 0.90).
    2. Every pitch wears down the UCL a little. More torque wears it down much faster.
    3. Calibrated to ~49 MLB UCL injuries a year: +10% torque ≈ 2× the pitches.
